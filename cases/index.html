Host a night

Turn a Tuesday
into an event.

Everything a venue needs to run a dart night lives in ED OS on the machine and EDhub on your phone or laptop.

  1. 01

    Post the event.

    Create a store event in EDhub. It shows up on your machines and in the DARTMAN Games App, where players following your venue can find it.

  2. 02

    Run the league.

    Set up leagues and MATCH play, best of 3, 5 or 7 legs. Brackets and the champion go up on the big screen.

  3. 03

    Keep the room watching.

    Live ranking keeps every player in the race, and the dual cameras record highlight clips worth cheering for.

  4. 04

    End on a group photo.

    The machine counts down and snaps the whole crew. Players keep it in the app and share it from there.
