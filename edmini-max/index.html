Questions, answered.

Will it fit my space?

The board section is 5.5 in deep and the footprint is 22.8 × 13.8 in. Plan a play area of about 13 × 2.3 ft and a 7.9 ft ceiling. Check your wall

How hard is it to install?

It arrives in three cartons, each light enough for one person. Set the console on the base, clip on the panels, plug in, connect to Wi-Fi. Watch the whole thing in the installation film above.

What if something breaks?

EDmini is modular, so you replace only the part that breaks, without sending the machine back. It comes with a 12-month warranty.

Can I charge customers?

Yes. Charge per game or per hour, run happy hours, and add an optional coin or bill acceptor.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Shop Pay and Google Pay. Venues buying several machines can get a quote and invoice.

Do I need someone to run it?

No. Lock or open machines remotely, set pricing on the screen, and guests call staff with the service button.
