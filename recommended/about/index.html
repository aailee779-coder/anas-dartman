Where our marks come from

Three darts.
Three marks.

Darts players chalk their hits. One player throws three darts a round: one hit is a slash, two make an X, three close it with an O.

  • /

    / · One hit. The first mark of the round.

  • ×

    X · Two hits. The second dart crosses the slash.

  • O · Three hits. The third dart closes it with a circle.

  • Ox · Our face. The DARTMAN smiley is drawn from the same marks.
