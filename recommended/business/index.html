Business questions.

How long until it pays for itself?

It depends on foot traffic and pricing. Of 100+ partner venues, most reach positive cash flow within 3–6 months.1

Do I need dedicated staff?

No. Every machine can be controlled remotely; one person manages all of them.

What if a machine breaks?

Modular design: replace only the part that breaks. Remote diagnosis plus spare-part swaps keep downtime short.

How can I charge guests?

Per game, per time or happy hour, with an optional coin or bill acceptor.

Is there revenue data I can look at?

Leave your details below and we'll send a revenue estimate for your type of venue.

Can I put my brand on the machine?

No. EDmini machines carry the DARTMAN brand only. Magnetic skins change the look of the target panel.
