Buying EDmini Max

How much is EDmini Max?

$3,999 with free shipping and a 12-month warranty.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Major cards and wallets at checkout.

Can I get a quote for several machines?

Yes. Use the partnership inquiry.

Set-up & service

Is EDmini easy to install?

One person assembles it in about 30 minutes.1 It ships in three cartons.

How much space does it need?

About 0.2 m² of floor; plan a play area of 400 × 70 cm with a 240 cm ceiling.

What if the machine breaks?

Modular design: replace only the part that breaks, with remote diagnosis.

For venues

Do I need dedicated staff?

No. Every machine can be controlled remotely.

How long until it pays for itself?

Most partner venues reach positive cash flow within 3–6 months.1

Can I add my brand?

No. EDmini machines carry the DARTMAN brand only. Magnetic skins change the look of the target panel.

Playing & the app

Do I need darts experience?

No. Party games work on the first throw. See the Know Dart guide.

What is a DM account?

One account for the machine, the app and the web, with your stats and points. See Member.

How do I find a venue?

Use Where to play or the DARTMAN App.
