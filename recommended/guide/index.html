Know Dart

Your first game,
in five steps.

No darts experience needed. EDmini keeps the score for you.

  1. 1Log in

    Scan the QR code on the screen with the DARTMAN GAMES App, or just press play as a guest.

  2. 2Pick a game

    Party games are the easiest start: Bowling, Balloon or Soccer PK. Up to 8 players.

  3. 3Stand behind the line

    Keep your toes behind the throw line. Soft-tip darts only.

  4. 4Throw three darts

    Each turn is three darts. The board scores every hit and lights it up.

  5. 5Change player

    Press the button or wait for the screen, then pass the darts on.

How to count scores

Where you hit
is what you score.

  • Single zone ×1. Hit 5, score 5. The biggest area, so the easiest to hit.
  • Double zone ×2. Hit 5, score 10. Smaller than the single zone.
  • Triple zone ×3. Hit 5, score 15. The smallest and the hardest.
  • Bullseye. The inner and outer bull carry a high value, such as 50 points.

Why does everyone aim for the bull? The highest score is not the bull, it's triple 20. The bull is just bigger, so it's easier.

Dart grips

How you hold it changes where it lands.

  • 1

    Pencil grip

    Hold the dart like a pencil, the barrel between thumb and index finger. The other fingers rest lightly for support.

  • 2

    Three-finger grip

    Thumb, index and middle finger on the barrel. A looser feel during the throw.

  • 3

    Four-finger grip

    Thumb on one side, three fingers on the other. A stable hold.

Dart stance

Balanced, natural, behind the line.

Keep both feet flat and anchored to the floor, your foot against the throw line.

  • Beginners

    Middle stance

    Lead foot at about 45° to the throw line. The back foot at a slight angle, carrying about 10% of your weight on the toe.

  • Stable

    Side-on stance

    Stand completely sideways. Many players like it for stability and easy alignment.

  • Natural

    Front-facing stance

    Face the board with both feet pointing forward. Comfortable if you naturally face forward.

  • The rule

    Don’t step over the line

    Always stand behind the throw line, never on or over it.

Throwing darts

Four movements.

  1. 1Set-up

    Face the board, aim with both eyes and keep your body balanced and comfortable.

  2. 2Pull back

    Move the arm from the elbow, like folding a fan. Palm naturally up, wrist relaxed.

  3. 3Release

    Let go like a paper plane, without moving the elbow. Release all fingers at once for a stable flight.

  4. 4Follow through

    Extend the arm straight after the release, as if pointing at the target with your fingertips.

Game rules

Know the rules, then have fun.

  • Zero One

    01 games

    301, 501, 701, 901, 1101 and 1501: they all end in “01”. 301 and 501 are best for beginners.

    Everyone starts at the same score and subtracts every hit. The first to reach exactly 0 wins.

    Go below zero and it’s a bust: the score goes back to where it was before the turn. Left with 32 and hit the 50 bull? Bust, still 32.

  • 15–20 + Bull

    Cricket

    Only 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20 and the bull count.

    3 marks open a number: three singles, a single and a double, or one triple. Then every hit there scores.

    When your opponent also gets 3 marks, the number is closed for everyone. Highest score when all numbers are closed, or at the round limit, wins.

  • Easiest

    Count-Up

    Everyone starts at 0 and adds up three darts a round.

    The highest score after 8 rounds wins.

Darts & accessories

Know the equipment.

  • House darts

    Darts are provided

    Most places with dart machines give guests house darts. Bringing your own? Check the venue has the right kind of board.

  • Soft tip vs steel tip

    Two kinds of darts

    Soft tip: a plastic tip for electronic boards with sensors. Casual and home play.

    Steel tip: a sharp steel point for traditional sisal boards. Common in professional play.

  • Parts of a dart

    Tip · Barrel · Shaft · Flight

    Tip sticks in the board; it wears and is replaced often. Barrel is where you hold it and the centre of balance: pick a grip texture that suits you. Shaft connects the flight to the barrel and steadies the path. Flight is the wing at the back that keeps the dart stable.

Distance rules

Hang it right. Stand right.

  • 173 cmBoard heightFrom the floor to the centre of the bullseye.
  • 244 cmSoft tip throw distanceFrom the front of the board (not the wall) to the throw line.
  • 237 cmSteel tip throw distanceFrom the front of the board (not the wall) to the throw line.
