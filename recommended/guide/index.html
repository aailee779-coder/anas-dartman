Know Dart

Your first game,
in five steps.

No darts experience needed. EDmini keeps the score for you.

  1. 1Log in

    Scan the QR code on the screen with the DARTMAN App, or just press play as a guest.

  2. 2Pick a game

    Party games are the easiest start: Bowling, Balloon or Soccer PK. Up to 8 players.

  3. 3Stand behind the line

    Keep your toes behind the throw line. Soft-tip darts only.

  4. 4Throw three darts

    Each turn is three darts. The board scores every hit and lights it up.

  5. 5Change player

    Press the button or wait for the screen, then pass the darts on.

Scoring

Where you hit
is what you score.

  • Single. The big segments score the number on the rim.
  • Double. The thin outer ring scores twice the number.
  • Treble. The thin inner ring scores three times the number.
  • Bull. The centre. Score depends on the game settings.

Two classics to learn first.

  • 01 games

    Everyone starts at 301 (or 501, 701…) and counts down to exactly zero. The first player to hit zero wins.

    See 01 games
  • Cricket

    Hit 15 to 20 and the bull three times each to close them. Close all and lead on points to win.

    See Cricket
  • Just for fun

    Party games need no rules at all. Throw, laugh, pass the darts.

    See party games
