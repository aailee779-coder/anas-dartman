EDmini

Get the highlights.

  • Finishes. Black, gold, silver, pink, orange. Pick the one that suits your venue.

  • Ultra-thin. The machine body is 14 cm deep, and the whole machine needs just 0.2 m² of floor. It stands against the wall, out of the way.

  • Two screens. A 21.5″ touch screen for smooth, intuitive play, and a 21.5″ upper monitor so the whole room can follow the score.

  • Illuminated target. Soft-tip, backlit LED segments with switchable colours that light up every hit.

  • Dual cameras. One above the upper screen, one in the bar under the target. They record the best moments of every game, ready to share.

  • Surround sound. Four-channel audio puts the whole room inside the game.

  • Payment. A standard coin acceptor on the side of the cabinet, with a bill acceptor available as an option. Charge per game, per time, or run a happy hour.

  • Custom magnetic skins. Wrap the whole machine in your own design. Skins snap on in seconds.

Questions? Answers.

How do I buy EDmini Max?

Order at the DARTMAN Shop, with free shipping and a 12-month warranty. Venues and distributors buying several machines can ask for a quote.

Is EDmini easy to install?

Unbox, plug in, connect to the network and switch on. One person assembles it in about 10 minutes.1

How much space does it need?

The machine needs about 0.2 m² of floor. Plan a play area of 400 × 70 cm with a 240 cm ceiling.

What if the machine breaks?

Modular design: replace only the part that breaks. Remote diagnosis plus direct spare-part swaps keep downtime short.

Do I need dedicated staff to manage it?

No. Every machine can be controlled remotely; one person manages all of them.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Major cards and wallets at checkout. A standard coin acceptor lets guests pay at the machine; a bill acceptor is optional.

How long until it pays for itself?

It depends on foot traffic and pricing. Of 100+ partner venues, most reach positive cash flow within 3–6 months.1 See Business
