Questions? Answers.

How much is EDmini Max?

$3,999 with free shipping and a 12-month warranty. Venues and distributors buying several machines can ask for a quote.

Is EDmini easy to install?

Unbox, plug in, connect to the network and switch on. One person assembles it in about 30 minutes.1

How much space does it need?

The machine needs about 0.2 m² of floor. Plan a play area of 400 × 70 cm with a 240 cm ceiling.

What if the machine breaks?

Modular design: replace only the part that breaks. Remote diagnosis plus direct spare-part swaps keep downtime short.

Do I need dedicated staff to manage it?

No. Every machine can be controlled remotely; one person manages all of them.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Major cards and wallets at checkout. An optional coin or bill acceptor lets guests pay at the machine.

How long until it pays for itself?

It depends on foot traffic and pricing. Of 100+ partner venues, most reach positive cash flow within 3–6 months.1 See Business
