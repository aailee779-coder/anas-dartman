Score marks,

Darts players keep score with a slash, an x and an o. We made those three marks the DARTMAN language, and the ox face its first character.

  • O · Connect people. The board is the excuse. Friends, rivals and strangers are the point.

  • /

    Slash · Trendy & innovative. A machine that belongs in a modern room.

  • Ox · Easy for all. First-timers and league regulars, same machine, same night.
