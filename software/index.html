ED OS · on the machine

Plays smart.

A new interface on two 21.5-inch screens. Players are in a game in a few taps; owners set prices, hours and events right on the machine.

For players

  • QR

    Join in a scan. Add players by scanning a QR code with the app, or jump in as a guest. No account needed to play.

  • 1st

    Live ranking. Scores and standings update with every dart, so the whole table knows who’s ahead.

  • Group photo. The machine’s camera snaps the whole team together.

  • Highlight recording. Two cameras catch the big moments for players to replay and share in the app.

  • OTA

    Always up to date. New features and fixes arrive over the air. No service visit needed.

For owners

  • Charge per game. Set the credits for each game type, or reset everything to default in one tap.

  • Or charge by time. Switch on time billing for groups that want the machine for an hour or more.

  • Happy hour. Schedule discount periods for each day of the week.

  • Reservations and events. Block time for table reservations and events from the same screen.

  • Service bell. Players call staff from the machine; the call shows up for your team.

  • Operations report. See the credits collected by the machine, and who logged in to the back end and when.

DARTMAN Games App · in your pocket

Your game goes home with you.

Scan the machine to log in and your settings come with you. Afterwards, every stat, badge and highlight lives on your phone.

App screens: log in, LED effect settings, a stats chart, pinned badges and the shop.

  • Profile

    Your player card with stats, trends and win rate, in themed skins.

  • Badges

    Collect achievement badges and pin your favorites to your profile.

  • Highlights

    Witness your MVP moments: a personal highlight reel, so every game-winning shot is remembered.

  • Challenges

    Complete missions in the app and claim surprise in-game gift packs.

  • Themes

    Personalized themes, game effects and sounds for your profile.

  • Rankings

    Global, monthly and friends leaderboards for every game.

  • Find a store

    A map of venues with DARTMAN machines. Follow your home bar.

  • Events

    See what your venues have on, straight from their EDhub.

  • Friends & gifts

    Follow players you meet at the bar and send them gifts.

  • Scan to log in

    Scan the code on the machine and your settings sync to it.
