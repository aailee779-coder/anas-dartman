Good questions.

Straight answers for venue owners and players. Can’t find yours? Ask us

Is EDmini Max hard to install?

No. It ships in 3 cartons, the modular parts are light enough for one person to set up, and the whole kit fits in a car (we tested it with a Honda Civic). Unbox, assemble, plug in, connect to Wi-Fi and power on. Typical setup time to confirm.

What happens if something breaks?

EDmini is modular: replace the part that failed, not the whole machine. Common fixes, like swapping a target segment, are covered step by step in the user manual. For software issues, use the Report button in ED OS; for hardware, contact your authorized distributor or sales representative.

Do I need someone to look after it?

Not full time. From EDhub on your phone or laptop you can see every machine’s live status, lock or unlock remotely, answer service calls, set pricing and happy hour, and give staff their own permissions. Software updates arrive over the air.

How long until it pays for itself?

It depends on your foot traffic and how you price games. We don’t publish a one-size number. Tell us about your venue and we’ll prepare an estimate on request.

Request an estimate

How do players pay?

Fit a coin acceptor or a bill acceptor (optional). You set the pricing in ED OS, per game or by time, and EDhub supports machine booking by the hour or by credit. Card and app payments in the US to confirm.

How much space do I need?

The machine stands 58 × 35 cm (22.8 × 13.8 in). Plan a play area of about 400 × 70 cm (13 × 2.3 ft), a ceiling of at least 240 cm (7.9 ft), and 25 cm (10 in) between machines.

What comes in the box?

12 darts, 3 spare flights, 50 soft tips, a throw line and the user manual.

What does the warranty cover?

12 months. Darts and their parts (tips, barrels, shafts, flights) are consumables and are not covered. Warranty details
